# Google Business Profile checklist (service-area business)

I cannot see or edit BrightView's Google Business Profile, so **nothing here has been verified or changed**. Every step needs the owner (profile access). Do **not** create a second profile: Google allows one profile per service-area business.

Official guidance: [Representing your business](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177) · [Service areas](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481) · [Address](https://support.google.com/business/answer/2853879) · [Eligibility](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036)

## 1. Find and claim (owner)
- [ ] Search for "BrightView LLC" while signed in to the owner's Google account; open the existing profile and choose **Claim/Manage** if unclaimed. Do not add a new listing.
- [ ] Complete verification (Google chooses the method: video, phone, email or postcard).

## 2. Business type and address (owner)
- [ ] Set the business as a **service-area business**: it travels to customers and does not serve them at its own address.
- [ ] **Hide the address:** Edit profile → Location → turn off "show address to customers". The home address is then used only for verification and is not shown.
- [ ] Service areas: add only areas actually served. Google asks that the whole area stay within roughly 2 hours of driving from where the business is based. Enter areas as regions/cities the owner confirms; the website says "Southeast Michigan" and must stay consistent.

## 3. Core details (owner)
- [ ] Name exactly "BrightView LLC" (no added keywords: keyword-stuffed names violate guidelines).
- [ ] Phone (810) 964-6975; website = the confirmed canonical domain.
- [ ] Primary category = the service that brings most revenue. Pick from Google's current list in the dashboard (the list changes; categories such as "Window cleaning service" and "Pressure washing service" are the likely candidates; verify they exist). Add secondary categories only for services truly offered (gutters, roof/soft washing, holiday lighting if a suitable category exists).
- [ ] Add each service with a short, honest description matching the website (no prices unless confirmed).
- [ ] Business description (750 characters max): family-owned, Michigan, the six services, free quotes. No promotions, URLs or unverified claims.
- [ ] Hours: add only if there are real, consistent hours; otherwise leave unset.

## 4. Photos (owner)
- [ ] Upload real project photos: before/after, finished work, holiday displays, the team at work, the logo and a cover image. Use the same photos that are on the website gallery. Prefer new photos regularly.

## 5. Reviews (owner)
- [ ] Create the short review link (Profile → Ask for reviews) and send it to satisfied customers by text or email after a job. Never offer incentives and never write or buy reviews.
- [ ] Reply to every review, positive or negative, politely and without private details.
- [ ] The Facebook recommendations on the website are separate; they do not count as Google reviews.

## 6. Consistency (citations)
- [ ] Use the same name, phone and website everywhere: Facebook (already live), Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, Yelp, Nextdoor and any directory listings (HomeAdvisor, Angi, Thumbtack, etc.). Hide or omit the home address on every directory that allows it.

## 7. Ongoing
- [ ] Weekly or biweekly **Updates/Posts** (seasonal offers like holiday lighting, project highlights).
- [ ] Answer questions; keep services and photos current; check insights monthly (calls, website clicks).
- [ ] Link the website, and later add the Search Console and GBP views to the same Google account.

## What the website already supports
- Visible, consistent business name/phone/email, an approximate (non-exact) service area, real project photos, crawlable service pages (from PR 2), and structured data without any street address or coordinates.
