# Research notes for BrightView service descriptions (October 2026)

The public sales copy lives in src/content/service-details.ts. These references support general homeowner benefits, **not** unverified claims that BrightView uses particular chemicals, equipment, methods, warranties, memberships or certifications.

## Window Cleaning
- Andersen Windows: dust, film, dirt and surface deposits can be cleaned from windows; materials and coatings require care. https://parts.andersenwindows.com/items_Care_GlassClean.html
- U.S. Department of Energy: windows provide natural daylight, one major value of the window itself. Do not claim a measured energy saving from washing the glass. https://www.energy.gov/articles/energy-101-daylighting

## Pressure Washing
- EPA: dirty wash water can carry pollutants; don't claim environmental or water safety benefits without verified practices. https://www.epa.gov/npdes/stormwater-discharges-municipal-sources
- Avoid claiming complete stain removal or a like-new finish on every material.

## Soft Washing
- James Hardie: some exterior siding can be damaged by pressure washing. Manufacturer recommends low-pressure water for Hardie siding. Do not assume specific BrightView chemical treatment or all siding types are approved. https://www.jameshardie.com/blog/siding-durability/tips-for-cleaning-your-siding/

## Roof Cleaning
- Asphalt Roofing Manufacturers Association (ARMA): some roof discoloration is algae; high-pressure washing can damage asphalt shingles. Manufacturer-compatible methods must be considered for individual roofs. https://www.asphaltroofing.org/algae-discoloration-of-roofs/
- Avoid claims about extending roof life or guaranteed stain removal.

## Gutter Cleaning
- National Weather Service: clear clogged gutters and downspouts to help rainwater flow. https://www.weather.gov/mob/wind_mitigation
- ARMA: keep roof gutters clean to promote drainage. https://www.asphaltroofing.org/readying-your-roof-for-extreme-weather-events/
- Don't promise to prevent flooding or repair damaged gutter systems.

## Holiday Lighting
- Business fact confirmed by owner: BrightView supplies its own lights. Existing website copy states installation and post-season takedown.
- Electrical Safety Foundation International: exterior lights entail weather, ladders and electrical precautions. https://www.esfi.org/outdoor-decoration-safety-tips/
- Do not claim permanent light ownership, storage, replacement bulbs, maintenance, custom fabrication, exact light types or warranties without owner verification.

## Claims to verify with BrightView before using
Interior-window and screen cleaning; stain restoration; chemical products and soft-wash/roof cleaning treatment recipes; full downspout cleaning; roof types serviced; light storage/maintenance/ownership; warranty, service area, insurance, certifications and exact package details.
