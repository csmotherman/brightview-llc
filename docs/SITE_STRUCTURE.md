## Services overview decisions

- Three distinct editorial sections rather than repeating the homepage card grid.
- Existing BrightView project photos: window after, pressure-washing porch and holiday-lighting house.
- Internal index quickly jumps visitors to the relevant service on the same page.
- Detail-page links work but lead to intentionally blank placeholders until each page is designed.
- Footer quote button opens BrightView's existing Facebook profile in a new tab; no quote form or third-party integration.
- No unverified specific work inclusions, service-area cities, reviews, credentials, insurance or pricing.
- Keep the site noindex until launch review.
