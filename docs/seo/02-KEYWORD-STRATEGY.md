# Keyword and page strategy (qualitative: no volumes or rankings are claimed)

Keywords below are **search intents customers plausibly use**, not measured demand. Validate them in Google Search Console after launch (queries report) and, if desired, in Google Keyword Planner.

## Service landing pages (one URL per distinct intent)

| Page | Primary intent | Supporting phrases | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/services/window-cleaning` | window cleaning, window washing | window cleaning near me, residential window cleaning | Windows only; do not claim screens/tracks/interior unless confirmed |
| `/services/pressure-washing` | pressure washing, power washing | pressure washing near me, driveway/patio/paver cleaning, fence cleaning | "Power washing" and "pressure washing" are the same intent: one page, both terms |
| `/services/soft-washing` | soft washing, house washing | soft wash house, low-pressure siding cleaning | Distinct from pressure washing: surfaces that need a gentler method |
| `/services/roof-cleaning` | roof cleaning, roof washing | roof stain / algae streak removal | Keep to existing cautious wording (material-dependent) |
| `/services/gutter-cleaning` | gutter cleaning | gutter cleaning near me, clean clogged gutters | Do not promise flood prevention or repairs |
| `/services/holiday-lighting` | Christmas light installation, holiday lighting | holiday light installation near me, Christmas light hanging and takedown | Confirmed: BrightView supplies the lights, installs, removes |

## Supporting pages
- `/services` hub: links to all six (crawlable anchors) and keeps the accordion.
- `/service-areas`: regional page (Southeast Michigan). No city pages without confirmed, unique local content (see below).
- `/gallery`, `/about`, `/reviews`, `/faq`, `/contact`, `/quote`: trust and conversion pages.

## Local targeting rules
- Only the approved geographic wording "Southeast Michigan" is used publicly.
- **City or county names are not published** until the owner confirms each one. A confirmed city earns its own page only if there is unique, verifiable content (real projects there, local specifics). Pages that merely swap the city name are doorway pages and violate Google's spam policy.
- Recommended order if cities are confirmed: first list them (with project photos) on `/service-areas`; split into separate pages only when each has enough unique substance.

## Content quality guardrails
Never invent pricing, response times, chemicals, equipment, guarantees, licenses, insurance, certifications, ratings or supported materials. Each service page must satisfy a distinct intent with distinct copy.
